About Me

I’m Matthew Ward, a student in the Applied and Computational Mathematics Emphasis (ACME) program at Brigham Young University. I was previously a member of the BYU Biophysics Group and a data engineer intern working on Geiger-mode lidar at 3DEO, Inc.

I focus on computer vision (especially for 3-dimensional imaging these days), optimization, and the mathematics behind data science and machine learning.
